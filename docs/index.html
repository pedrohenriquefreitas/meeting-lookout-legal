Meeting Lookout

YOUR NEXT MEETING, IN SIGHT

A macOS menu bar app that turns calendar meetings into reminders you genuinely cannot miss — built for people with ADHD, time blindness, or deep-focus work.

  • Large, branded alerts centered on every connected monitor — over full-screen apps and across Spaces
  • Staged reminders (10/5/3/1 minutes, plus your own custom times), snooze, and one-click meeting join
  • A live countdown to your next meeting right in the menu bar
  • Works with Apple Calendar — including Google, Outlook.com, and Exchange accounts added to macOS
  • Private by design: no analytics, no accounts, no servers. Your calendar never leaves your Mac.
