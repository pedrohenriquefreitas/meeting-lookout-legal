Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version: Meeting Lookout collects no data. Your calendar information is read on your Mac, stays on your Mac, and is never uploaded, logged, or shared. There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers.

1. Who we are

Meeting Lookout is a macOS menu bar app by Pedro Freitas that shows prominent, multi-monitor reminders for upcoming calendar meetings. This policy covers the Mac App Store edition of the app.

2. Information the app reads (and never sends anywhere)

With your explicit permission through Apple's Calendar privacy prompt, the app reads from Apple Calendar:

  • Event titles and start times — to schedule and display your reminders
  • Event location, notes, and URL fields — only to detect a video-conference join link (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex) so the reminder can show a Join button
  • Calendar names — so you can choose which calendars to watch

All of this processing happens locally on your Mac. The app has no server, makes no network requests of its own, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting frameworks.

3. Information stored on your Mac

  • Preferences — your reminder times and settings, stored in the app's local preferences
  • Reminder history — a small local file of anonymized (hashed) meeting identifiers and reminder states, kept so reminders are not repeated after a restart. It contains no event titles, notes, or links and is pruned automatically within 24 hours after each meeting.

Deleting the app removes this data.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Data we collect

None. We collect, sell, and share no personal data. The App Store privacy label for Meeting Lookout is "Data Not Collected."

6. Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and is rated for general audiences.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
