Support

Common questions

  • No meetings appear: Open the Setup Guide from the menu bar icon and confirm Calendar access is granted, then check which calendars are selected under Calendars. All-day events never trigger timed reminders.
  • Where are my work meetings? If your company allows it, add your work account in System Settings > Internet Accounts so its events appear in Apple Calendar. If your company blocks this, the app cannot see those meetings.
  • Reminders are locked: Timed reminders need an active subscription or trial. Open Upgrade… from the menu — both plans start with a 1-week free trial. Use Restore Purchases on a new Mac.
  • A reminder didn't fire: Check that Watch for meetings is on, the meeting's calendar is selected, and the reminder time wasn't already past when the event was created.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription: App Store app → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions, or the Manage Subscription link in the app's Upgrade window.
